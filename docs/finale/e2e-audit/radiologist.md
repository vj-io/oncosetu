# E2E audit: radiologist

Persona: a radiologist who uses PACS every day and has never seen OncoSetu. I asked for one place where all of a patient's scans and reports are easy to find, open and compare. Signed in as the clinician (Dr. Meera Iyer) through the demo role picker. Tested on headless Chromium at 1366x768 and at 375x812 (mobile emulation). Only http://localhost:3000 was used.

Screenshots: `docs/finale/e2e-audit/shots/radiologist/`. Scripts: `C:\Users\GARV BANSAL\Documents\onco-audit\radiologist\`.

## (a) First impression

From the patient list, the patient's name takes me to an Overview that is long and text-heavy. The **All records** tab is where a radiologist should go. The tab name is plain, and the tab shows a count, so I found it in one click. Inside, the page is close to what I want. One date-ordered list mixes reports and scans, with year dividers. Kind chips carry counts (All 14 · Reports 7 · Scans 5 …). A "Kind of scan" dropdown is built from the modalities this patient actually has (CT/MRI/PET-CT/Nuclear for Mr Khan; X-ray/Mammogram/Ultrasound for Mrs Deshmukh). There is also a Newest/Oldest toggle. Scan rows tell me the image count and whether a report is linked. I could reach any of Mr Khan's five studies in about four clicks, comfortably under 10 seconds: patient → All records → Scans → row.

Viewing is where it starts to feel unlike a viewer:
- The preview panel is not sticky. When I pick a study low in the list, the preview stays at the top of the page, off-screen.
- The panel scrolls inside itself, so the page scrolls inside a page.
- Once I scroll down to the image, neither the patient's name nor any registered identity is visible. The only name on screen is the one in the file header.
- The image is a fixed ~330 px square. The mouse wheel does not step through a series, and there is no enlarge or full-screen view.

The words are disciplined, and that is good. Every image says it is not read. The comparison is labelled exactly "Earlier study, for comparison. OncoSetu does not read images." I found no "change", "progression", measuring or annotation anywhere.

The scan↔report pairing works and is honest ("Possible match … A person has to confirm it"). Two things undercut it:
- Once I click **Not a match**, I cannot undo it.
- The scan then says "The written report is not on file", which is untrue: the report is on file, and I only said it is not this scan's report.

The wrong-patient X-ray is correctly kept out of All records. When I open it, though, the other man's chest X-ray is shown large under Sunita Deshmukh's name, and the mismatch explanation sits below the fold.

## (c) What worked well

- **Order and toggle**: newest first by default with year dividers. "Newest first" switches to "Oldest first", the list reverses correctly, and the order is kept in the URL (`?order=old`), so it survives a reload.
- **Counts match**: Khan has All 14 = Reports 7 + Scans 5 + Labs 1 + Prescriptions 1 + Other 0. Sunita has 13 = 7 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 0. Modality filters gave CT 2, MRI 1, PET-CT 1, Nuclear 1. Filters are kept in the URL.
- **Keyboard**: ↑/↓ and ←/→ move between papers, and Previous/Next work with "n of N". When the series slider has focus, arrows, Home and End step frames (1 of 9 … 9 of 9) without changing paper.
- **Pairing state**: "Possible match" shows until a person acts. **Confirm** becomes "Report linked / A person confirmed these belong together", with **Unlink**, and the report page shown next to the scan. **Not a match** persisted across reload, and the rejected report was not proposed again for that scan or any other.
- **Comparison wording**: exactly "Earlier study, for comparison. OncoSetu does not read images." I found no measuring, annotation, "change", "progression", "stable", "interval" or "increase" text anywhere on the scan pages.
- **Held, quarantined and duplicate files** were kept out of All records. Sunita's wrong-patient X-ray, two handwritten prescriptions (one flagged as a possible duplicate) and a held histopathology report are all counted in a notice and not listed. Opening the held X-ray by `#doc=` falls back to the first listed paper instead of showing it.
- **Documents tab** groups by kind, with a "Scans, ECG and video" section. Every scan card shows the file header name, age and sex, so a mismatch is easy to spot there.
- **Source viewer for reports** shows the page image, the extracted line with UNVERIFIED status, and a header-versus-registered identity table.
- **No sideways scroll at 375 px** on records, preview, side by side, documents, the held X-ray or the report viewer (scrollWidth = 375). Only the tab strip scrolls, inside itself.
- **No console errors or 4xx/5xx** in any run.

## What a radiologist would still expect (no interpretation involved)

- A sticky preview with an identity strip (RAD-03, RAD-04).
- Wheel scrolling through a series, a larger view, and linked scrolling as an option in side by side (RAD-09, RAD-10).
- Study description, accession number or number of series in the header block. Today the block shows type, date, body part, institution, name, sex, age and image count only.
- Filtering by body part (chest, breast, brain), not only by modality, and modality filters that bring in the matching reports (RAD-13).
- Seeing a candidate report next to the scan before confirming (RAD-12).
