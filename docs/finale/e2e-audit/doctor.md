# E2E audit: the oncologist (Dr. Meera Iyer)

Auditor role: a busy oncologist on a hospital PC (1366x768), about 3 minutes per patient, no prior knowledge of the app.
Run: 19 Nov 2026, against http://localhost:3000 only, headless Chromium (Playwright). Scripts: `C:\Users\GARV BANSAL\Documents\onco-audit\doctor\`. Screenshots: `docs/finale/e2e-audit/shots/doctor/`.

What I changed in the shared demo data, all through the UI: I verified one item for Khan (the addendum line "The diagnosis in the original report dated 22/01/2026 is unchanged."). I sealed **Lakshmi Narayanan's** visit, not Khan's, so the other auditors keep Khan's What's new list. I sent one study form with made-up answers, then signed out. I opened Resolve, Dispute and Edit and cancelled each one without saving.

## (a) First impression

Signing in takes one click on "Dr. Meera Iyer", then Sign in. The patients list is clean. For each patient it shows age and sex, how many items are new, how many papers are missing, and when the last visit was sealed. Within 5 seconds I could see that Khan has 22 new items and 8 missing papers.

On Khan's Overview, the "First look" block is the best part of the app. Six cards say what is new, what is missing, what is held, where sources disagree, what is unchecked and when the last seal was made. Every line links to a quote. Within 30 seconds I knew a PET-CT report and May blood tests had arrived, that the MRI, bone scan and CT reports were missing, and that the patient's Lapatinib does not match the printed prescription. That is exactly what I need before the patient sits down.

Then the trust problems start:
- **The numbers do not agree.** The What's new tab says 22. First look says 21. The chip inside What's new says New 17, and that section lists 15. I stopped trusting the counts.
- **The Overview is too long:** 3,907 px for Khan.
- **Date warnings are everywhere.** Almost every new line has a yellow "DAY AND MONTH COULD BE SWAPPED" tag. That includes 12/05/2026, whose swapped reading (5 Dec 2026) is still in the future. After the third one I stopped reading the tags.
- **The top diagnosis line is not the pathology.** It reads "Carcinoma colon - adjuvant chemotherapy Last cycle: Cycle 1 on 10/02/2026", taken from a day-care sheet, with the next column glued on. The real histopathology diagnosis is hidden behind "Show all 4".

The source viewer is good once you reach it: page image, word view and a highlighted quote. But at 1366x768 the paper starts about 640 px down, so I always had to scroll first. In the fact list, three lines are all called "Margins as written · Margins as written", so I could not tell them apart without opening each one.

For Sunita (first visit, disclosure unknown) the neutral-wording banner is clear and well placed. But the first "new" item shown is a 2024 mammography impression: "BI-RADS 3 (probably benign)… short-interval follow-up suggested". For a woman now on chemotherapy for breast cancer, that is a misleading first line.

Nowhere did the app interpret a scan, recommend anything, give a risk score or use "progression" or "change" wording. Scans are always labelled "stored, not read". No page scrolls sideways at 1366 px. There were no console errors and no 4xx/5xx responses except the 404s I triggered on purpose.

## (c) What worked well

- **First look:** six cards answer "what's new, what's missing, what's unchecked, what disagrees" on the first screen. Each line links to its quote or checklist row.
- **Traceability:** every fact I opened had the exact quote, the document and the page, and "Show the exact words" landed on the right document with the fact open. All 40+ fact links on Khan's Overview returned 200.
- **Conflict:** the conflict is shown side by side, "Patient said: Tab. Lapatinib 250 mg" against "Prescription: Capecitabine", with "a clinician decides". The Resolve dialog requires a note and deletes nothing.
- **Verification:** verifying is one click and records whether the source was opened ("SOURCE OPENED BY YOU"). Edit, Dispute and Reject each require a reason and keep the original.
- **Neutral wording:** Sunita's disclosure banner ("Use neutral wording with the patient and family") is clear. The list shows a disclosure icon for every patient.
- **Scans:** everywhere they read "Imaging file, stored, not read". I found no interpretation, recommendation, risk scoring, or "progression"/"change" wording about scans. A word scan of every page found none of "recall", "ACC.", "text layer", "progression" or "recommend". The only hit was a "METHOD" column header in the consent log, which is acceptable.
- **Seal:** the dialog lists checked/unchecked/disputed/documents/missing before sealing. The "Check the seal" page is plain-language, and "See the record as it was then" works.
- **Layout and errors:** no sideways scroll on any of the 26 page types at 1366 px. No console errors and no 4xx/5xx except deliberate 404s. Invalid patient ids and invalid document ids give a branded 404, not an error page.
- **Navigation and sign-out:** Previous/Next in the viewer shows "11 of 14, newest first" and moves correctly both ways. After sign-out, both /patients and Back land on /login.
