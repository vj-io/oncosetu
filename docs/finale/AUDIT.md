# OncoSetu: finale audit (19 Nov 2026)

Auditor: lead audit agent. Code at `main` f2ab1fe. Live site https://oncosetu-zeta.vercel.app, demo mode, synthetic data.

**How this was done.** I read README, DEMO, PITCH, HANDOFF, ARCHITECTURE and the parts of the PRD on the journeys and imaging. Then I signed in on the live site as all five staff roles and three patient/caregiver demo accounts, at 1366x768 and 375x812, and opened about 230 pages with Playwright. Screenshots are in `docs/finale/audit-shots/` (about 77 MB: **do not commit them all**, keep only the ones cited here). Nothing was written on live: no uploads, confirms, seals, letters, shares or settings. The one-tap demo sign-in and normal page views did write their usual session and audit rows.

**Write flows were tested on the local copy.** The coordinator's local copy (http://localhost:3000, its own Postgres on port 54600, demo seed loaded) ran the whole chain end to end with Playwright; shots are `flow-01` to `flow-15`:
1. Records made a request letter from Sunita's histopathology item. The hospital was prefilled and the letter printed in English and Hindi.
2. Rohan uploaded `hero__10` from the phone portal (it uploads as soon as the file is chosen).
3. Records saw "received · to confirm" and pressed Confirm closure; the request became FULFILLED.
4. The clinician sealed the visit, and `/verify` said "record unchanged".
5. The clinician made a share link; it opened on a phone with the passcode, and the signature was verified.
6. Records uploaded a CT series ZIP, a PET-CT ZIP, a PET-CT report PDF and an MRI DICOM to Khan. The viewer works, and the PET-CT report proposes closing his PET-CT item.
7. Uploading `hero__11` (IHC) after `hero__10`, which is the normal DEMO order, **reproduces the false "Two dates for one procedure" conflict** (P0-6).

Not tested: consent signing, erasure, desk OTP check-in, offline capture. **Never point a local copy at the live database:** HANDOFF §2 says local dev used to share the production Neon database.

## 1. Verdict per persona

**Patient / family (Rohan, on a phone, in Hindi).** Sign-in is one tap and the portal opens in Hindi with neutral "Setu Records" wording, which is exactly right. After that the home page works against him. It is 4,700 px tall on a phone. Six action cards and a list of every paper received come first, and the thing he needs most ("papers the clinic is still looking for, and where to ask") is at the very bottom. The "Send documents" card promises "Forward from WhatsApp or email", but the next page says WhatsApp is not switched on and email means "ask at the desk". Dates copied from his papers repeat, include dates long past ("dressing 17 Mar 2026", eight months ago), and sometimes print the raw date as the label ("02/02/2026 — 2 Feb 2026"). "Requests for papers" says "you can send them a letter", but there is no button to make one. A caregiver cannot ask for a letter for a missing paper, so the page is a dead end until staff act. "Share with a doctor" is blocked until a clinician seals a visit, so Sunita's family cannot share at all today. Privacy pages are complete but long (consent 4,800 px). Overall: safe and honest, but not yet obvious. The page should open with "what is missing, and where to ask", and every promise on a card should be true.

**Doctor / oncologist (five minutes between patients).** The landing page is the patient list, as promised. At 1366 px the table is wider than the screen, the only link to open a patient ("Open") is in the hidden last column, and the patient's name is not a link. Inside a patient, "First look" is good: new, missing, held, conflicting, unchecked, sealed, each one click away. Below it, though, the Overview grows to about 6,000 px. Every quote is printed twice (mono quote, then the same words again). Follow-up dates appear twice each. Dates show as "12 May 2026 or 5 Dec 2026", which reads like a bug. Missing papers are listed a second time, and in one place they contradict First look ("not in file" vs "received · to confirm"). A full row of "Go to" buttons repeats the 11 tabs. The thing the doctor asked for, **all reports and scans in date order, easy to open**, does not exist. Documents are grouped by type (pathology first, then imaging reports …) and sorted by *received* time inside each group. There is no date-ordered list, no filter, and no preview without leaving the page. Each document opens a source viewer that is heavy and technical ("Coverage: extraction recall for discharge summarys in evaluation 1.00", "ACC. 402179", "METHOD Typed PDF", "text layer"). Overall: a strong safety base, but too much to read and too many clicks for a five-minute slot.

**Clinical staff (desk, records, insurance desk, admin, auditor).** The pipeline exists and each step works in code. Desk lookup shows only initials until an OTP or witnessed consent. Held documents sit in their own red group with Reject / Attach. The missing-papers checklist has Request / Unobtainable / Dismiss and Confirm closure / Not a match. Letters can be printed in English and Hindi with a 7/21-day tracker. Seal and verify work, and the pre-auth worklist reads well. The gaps are about the **next step**. The records queue (Ravi's landing page) lists held documents and unchecked items but not *missing papers to request* or *letters due a reminder*, so the request-letter half of the loop is invisible until you open each patient. The study form `/measure` is linked from nowhere, even though the pitch depends on its numbers. The admin console has 12 sub-pages, and some cards contradict themselves ("10 of 10 rules in use; the rest are still being reviewed"). Settings shows an "Off: …" description next to a switch that reads ON. Overall: the pipeline is complete for a demo, but the records officer needs one "to do" list covering identity, checking **and** chasing papers.

**Radiologist.** In scope, the code is good. DICOM and ZIP studies (X-ray, CT, PET-CT, MRI, ultrasound cine, mammogram RLE) are stored. A middle-slice picture and a slice slider show, header fields appear "as written in the file", there is a "does not interpret images" line, and a wrong-patient DICOM header is held. **But no seeded demo patient has a single scan.** The 16 imaging samples in `public/demo-docs/imaging-manifest.json` (hero__12–18, returning__08–16) are never loaded by `scripts/seed-demo.ts`, so a judge can never see the imaging viewer on live. Scans fall into a separate "Scans, ECG and video" bucket and are never linked to their written report (the PET-CT report would sit under "Imaging reports" and the PET-CT study under "Scans"). There is no study list by date and modality, no previous scan of the same body part, and no report next to the images. Even the discharge summary's own line "CT thorax and abdomen dated 20/02/2026 was reviewed at admission" does not link to the CT report that is on file. Overall: the engine is ready, the experience is missing. Section 4 sets out a design that stays inside "stored, not read".

## 4. Radiologist imaging proposal: "All records"

**Principle: stored, not read.** OncoSetu shows *where* each scan and report is, *when* it was made and *what is written* on it (report text with quotes, DICOM header as written). It never measures, segments, compares pixels, or says "changed / progressed / stable". Pairing a scan with its report, and a scan with an earlier scan of the same body part, uses **header metadata and dates only**, is shown as "proposed", and a person can unlink it.

### 4.1 Route and layout
`/patients/[id]/records`, the new default tab for clinicians (Documents redirects here; the old grouped view stays as "Group by kind").


- **One list, document date newest first, year dividers.** If a document has no date, it is sorted by received date and labelled "date not on paper". An "Oldest first" toggle is offered.
- **Row:** icon (● report, ▣ scan, ✚ lab, ℞ prescription), date, kind (the radiology term for staff: "CT chest", "PET-CT whole body"), hospital, a checked count for reports (✓ 3/3) or a slice count for scans, and chips: *Amendment*, *Older record, newly received*, *Held*, *Synthetic*.
- **Click a row** to preview on the right without leaving the page (keyboard ↑/↓ moves through the list; Enter opens the full source viewer). On a phone the list is full-screen and a row opens the preview as a sheet with ← / → for previous and next.
- **Filters** are chips plus three selects, kept in the URL so links can be shared between staff (ids only, no PHI).

### 4.2 Scan ↔ report pairing (proposed, never asserted)
Propose a pair when the **report's own date equals the scan's study date (±3 days)** and **modality words match** (a report quoting "PET-CT" or "PET/CT" with study modality PT+CT; "CT chest" with CT and body part CHEST), preferring the same institution. Show "⧉ report" on the scan row and "⧉ scan" on the report row, with the reason ("same date, same kind of study"). A records officer or clinician can **Unlink**, and the audit row records the action. Unpaired scans show "Report not in file". This should feed Radar as a new rule `RAD-IMG-RPT-01`, "Report for the CT chest dated 10 Feb 2026 is not in file", so the missing-papers loop covers imaging too. A text mention ("CT thorax and abdomen dated 20/02/2026 was reviewed") links to the matching report or scan, or raises that item.

### 4.3 Prior studies (comparison without interpretation)
"Earlier scans of this body part" = same DICOM BodyPartExamined (or the same normalised study label) **and** a compatible modality, listed by date. **Compare side by side** opens two viewers next to each other, each with its own slider and header, and each with its own report quote underneath. There is no sync-by-anatomy, no overlay, no subtraction and no measurement. Stated plainly on the screen: "Side by side for your own reading. OncoSetu does not compare images."

### 4.4 Series navigation
A ZIP or CD export (DICOMDIR) may hold several series. A "Series" dropdown shows SeriesDescription as written (e.g. "AXIAL 5mm", "PET AC"), then the slice slider. The default slice is the middle one (already done). Cine (ultrasound) uses a play/pause button. Window and level presets are **out of scope** for now: the viewer is a "picture of the file", and the full DICOM is downloadable for the hospital PACS ("Download original (.dcm / .zip)", audited).

### 4.5 Data and API (no new clinical fields)
- `listRecords(tx, patientId)` → `{ id, kind: 'report'|'scan'|'lab'|'rx'|'other', docType, documentDate, receivedAt, institution, imaging?: { modality, bodyPart, studyDate, series: [{uid, description, instances}] }, checked, total, flags, pairedWith?: { id, reason, by: 'proposed'|'confirmed' } }`. Built from `documents` + `flags.imaging` (already stored).
- A new table `document_links (org_id, patient_id, a_id, b_id, kind 'scan_report'|'prior', state 'proposed'|'confirmed'|'rejected', decided_by, decided_at)` with RLS and FORCE, migration `00NN_document_links.sql`.
- The preview reuses `/api/documents/[id]/preview?frame=`; add `&series=`.
- Shares: the share payload may list scans as "CT chest · 10 Feb 2026 · stored at this clinic (not included)". No images leave via share links in v1.
